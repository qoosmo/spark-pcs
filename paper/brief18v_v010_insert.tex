\subsection{Specialized gates for the binary-field implementation}
\label{sec:specialized-gates}

For the production binary-field implementation we specialize each parent gate
to \(t^0=t\), \(t^1=t+1\). In characteristic two these points are distinct
deterministically. For inputs \(u,v\),
\[
a=u+t\,v,\qquad b=a+v=u+(t+1)v.
\]
The fold becomes
\[
w^0+(z+t)(w^1+w^0),
\]
because \(t^1-t^0=1\).

The Theorem~3.19 distance argument is unchanged. Double-parent constraints
force \(v=0\) and then \(u=0\). A singleton equation \(u+(t+b)v=0\) has
exactly one solution \(t\) when \(v\neq0\), hence probability \(1/q\), and
parents remain independent.

At \(n=20,k=2,i_0=3,s=407\), median prover time falls by \(17.4\%\) with one
Rayon thread and \(14.5\%\) with eight. Encoding multiplications fall exactly
from \(83{,}886{,}080\) to \(41{,}943{,}040\); folding removes
\(8{,}388{,}600\) base-field multiplications; commitment remains exactly
\(4{,}793{,}481\) Merkle hashes.

The pre-deduplication opening structure is unchanged. The deterministic v0.10
proof is \(592{,}577\) bytes, \(0.27\%\) larger than v0.9 because domain
separation changes query positions and therefore Merkle-path deduplication.

\paragraph{Linear proximity gap.}
For the optional GKL branch, Gao--Kan--Li applies for
\[
\delta\le1-(1-\Delta_C+\eta)^{1/3}
\]
and bounds the exceptional combining points by
\[
L+\frac{6}{\eta}+
\frac{2}{\eta((1-\Delta_C+\eta)^{1/3}-\sqrt{1-\Delta_C+\eta})}.
\]
This is the 1.5-Johnson result, not a full Johnson-radius claim.

\paragraph{The \(k=3\) record.}
The currently landable \(k=3\) point is \(i_0=2,s=277\). The \(s=261\) value
is conditional on a checked \(C_3=[64,8]\).

\paragraph{Transparent checked setup.}
The fixed domain-separated seed and first accepted public counter are immutable
protocol constants after the finite checked-prefix test. The checked-setup
failure budget is a one-time transparent-setup event, not a per-proof charge.
